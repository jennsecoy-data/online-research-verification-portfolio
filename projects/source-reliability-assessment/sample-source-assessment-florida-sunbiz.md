## Executive Summary

Sunbiz is the official website of the Florida Department of State, Division of Corporations.

It is a highly authoritative source for confirming what has been filed with the Division, including:

- Legal entity name appearing on the state record
- Florida document number
- Filing and effective dates
- State of domicile
- Filing status
- Registered agent information
- Listed officers, directors, managers, or authorized persons
- Annual-report filing history
- Filed corporate documents
- Fictitious-name registrations
- Selected liens, trademarks, and other Division records

However, Sunbiz should not automatically be treated as independent confirmation that every statement submitted by a filer is factually correct.

The Division of Corporations is an administrative filing agency. Documents that meet statutory filing requirements and include the required fees are generally accepted and indexed. The Division explains that filing a document does not determine whether the document is valid or whether the information it contains is correct.

Sunbiz is therefore:

- A primary source for what appears in Florida Division of Corporations records
- A strong source for filing history and entity-status research
- A useful source for locating copies of filed documents
- An incomplete source for determining whether a business is trustworthy, licensed, operational, financially stable, or compliant with requirements administered by another agency

Researchers should distinguish between:

> “This information appears in an official state filing.”

and:

> “This information has been independently verified as factually accurate.”

Those statements do not mean the same thing.

## Information Sunbiz Can Help Verify

### Business Entity Record

Depending on the entity type and available filings, Sunbiz may provide:

- Entity name
- Document number
- Entity type
- Filing date
- Effective date
- State or country of domicile
- Filing status
- Principal address
- Mailing address
- Registered agent name and address
- Officer, director, manager, or authorized-person information
- Annual-report filing dates
- FEI or EIN information when displayed
- Recent events or amendments
- Copies of filed documents

### Fictitious Names

Sunbiz may help confirm:

- Registered fictitious name
- Registration number
- Registration status
- Filing and expiration information
- Listed owner
- Owner address
- Related filed documents

A fictitious-name registration does not necessarily establish ownership of a trademark or exclusive rights to the name.

### Filed Documents

Sunbiz may provide images or copies of:

- Articles of Incorporation
- Articles of Organization
- Annual reports
- Amendments
- Reinstatements
- Dissolutions
- Registered-agent changes
- Mergers
- Conversions
- Statements of Fact
- Other documents maintained by the Division

The filed-document image is often more useful than the summary page because it preserves the information submitted at a particular point in time.

## Evaluation Criteria

### Authority

Sunbiz is operated by the Florida Department of State, Division of Corporations.

The Division is Florida’s official administrative filing authority for business entities, fictitious names, trademarks and service marks, selected liens, and other records within its jurisdiction.

For determining what is currently recorded with the Florida Division of Corporations, Sunbiz is an authoritative primary source.

**Assessment:** High

**Research decision:**

Use Sunbiz as the primary source for Florida Division of Corporations filing records.

### Accuracy

Sunbiz accurately presents records accepted and maintained by the Division.

However, the Division explains that submitted documents are generally accepted at face value when statutory filing requirements are met. Filing a document does not establish that every claim inside the filing is factually correct.

Possible issues include:

- Typographical errors submitted by the filer
- Outdated addresses
- Outdated officer or manager information
- Unauthorized or fraudulent filings
- Delayed corrections
- Names belonging to different people with similar identities
- Information that changed after the most recent filing
- Mailing addresses that are not physical operating locations

**Assessment:** High for confirming the official record; Moderate for confirming the underlying real-world facts

**Research decision:**

State findings as:

> “Sunbiz lists…”

or:

> “The Florida Division of Corporations record identifies…”

Avoid stating that filer-supplied information has been independently verified unless another reliable source supports it.

### Currency

The Division states that its database records are updated daily.

However:

- Recently submitted filings may still be processing.
- Document images may appear after the record itself is updated.
- A business may have changed information without filing the required update.
- Officer, manager, registered-agent, or address information may reflect the most recent filing rather than a real-time condition.
- An annual report confirms or updates information as of its filing date, not necessarily every day afterward.

**Assessment:** High for the latest processed Division record; Moderate for real-time operational information

**Research decision:**

Record:

- Date accessed
- Most recent event
- Most recent annual-report year
- Filing date
- Effective date
- Whether document images were available
- Whether more recent information was located elsewhere

### Relevance

Sunbiz is directly relevant when the research question involves:

- Florida entity registration
- State filing status
- Legal entity name on file
- Florida document number
- Registered agent
- Filed officers or managers
- Filing history
- Annual reports
- Corporate documents
- Fictitious-name records

Sunbiz is less relevant when the research question involves:

- Professional or occupational licensing
- Sales-tax registration
- Federal tax status
- Business complaints
- Pending lawsuits
- Customer reputation
- Financial condition
- Actual daily operations
- Product or service quality
- Insurance-plan authorization
- Health care licensing
- Local permits
- Ownership not clearly established in filed documents

**Assessment:** High for Division of Corporations records; Low for matters outside the Division’s jurisdiction

### Transparency

Sunbiz provides:

- Searchable entity records
- Filing-status information
- Document numbers
- Filing dates
- Annual-report histories
- Registered-agent information
- Officer or manager listings
- Images of many filed documents
- Search guides
- Filing instructions
- Definitions and FAQs
- Public explanations of the Division’s responsibilities and limitations

This transparency allows researchers to trace many findings to a specific filing.

**Assessment:** High

### Consistency

A Sunbiz summary page and its filed-document images may reflect information from different filing periods.

Differences can result from:

- New annual reports
- Amendments
- Registered-agent changes
- Reinstatement
- Dissolution
- Corrections
- Delayed image posting
- Historical information remaining visible in older documents

A difference is not automatically an error. Researchers should identify which filing is newest and whether the information serves a historical or current-record purpose.

**Assessment:** High when filing dates and document history are reviewed; Moderate when only the summary page is used

## Meaning of Common Sunbiz Fields

| Field | Appropriate interpretation | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Entity name | Name appearing on the Division’s record | Does not prove trademark ownership or that the business uses the name publicly |
| Document number | Identifier assigned by the Division | Not a federal EIN or professional-license number |
| Status | Status of the filing in Division records | Does not prove that the business is reputable, licensed, financially healthy, or actively serving customers |
| Date filed | Date the filing was accepted into the record | May differ from the entity’s effective date |
| Effective date | Date the entity’s legal existence or filing became effective when separately stated | Should not be assumed to equal the filing date |
| State | State or jurisdiction of domicile | Does not necessarily identify the primary operating location |
| Principal address | Address submitted as the principal address | May be a business office, residence, virtual office, or other submitted address |
| Mailing address | Address provided for mail | May not be a physical operating location |
| Registered agent | Person or organization designated to receive legal process | Is not necessarily an owner, employee, or customer-service contact |
| Officer or manager | Person listed in a filed record | May not prove current employment, ownership percentage, or operational authority |
| Annual report | Filing used to confirm or update entity information | Is not a financial statement |
| Active status | Entity has maintained active status in Division records | Does not confirm current operations, licensing, solvency, or regulatory compliance elsewhere |
| Certificate of status | Certification of entity status and fees due to the Division through a stated date | Does not certify overall business quality or compliance with every agency |

## What Sunbiz Does Not Establish by Itself

A Sunbiz record alone should not be treated as proof that:

- A business is currently operating
- A company has employees or customers
- A company is profitable or financially stable
- A business is trustworthy
- An address is a physical storefront
- A listed person knowingly consented to the filing
- An officer or manager still serves in that role
- A business holds required professional licenses
- A business is registered for all applicable taxes
- A business has no complaints
- A business has no pending lawsuits
- A company is authorized to provide regulated services
- A fictitious name is a registered trademark
- A business owns the website or phone number found elsewhere
- A business has complied with local permits or zoning requirements

Additional sources are required for those conclusions.

## Risks and Limitations

### Filer-Supplied Information

Many data points are submitted by businesses or representatives rather than independently researched by the Division.

### Fraudulent or Unauthorized Filings

The Division states that disputes and potentially fraudulent filings generally must be addressed by the affected parties or through the courts. A filed record may later be disputed or corrected.

### Jurisdiction Limitations

Sunbiz records apply to information held by the Florida Department of State, Division of Corporations.

Updating Sunbiz does not automatically update records held by:

- Florida Department of Revenue
- Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation
- Florida Agency for Health Care Administration
- Local governments
- Federal agencies
- Courts
- Licensing boards
- Other state agencies

### Filing Status Versus Business Condition

“Active” describes the status of the entity in the Division’s records. It should not be translated into claims such as:

- Open for business
- In good financial condition
- Properly licensed
- Free from complaints
- Compliant with every law
- Safe to hire
- Safe to invest in

### Historical Documents

Older filings remain valuable for historical research but may no longer describe the current entity.

### Similar Names

Search results may contain:

- Similar entity names
- Reinstated entities
- Inactive entities
- Fictitious names
- Foreign entities
- Separate businesses using related terms

Researchers should confirm the document number and other identifiers rather than relying on the name alone.

### Unofficial Websites

Third-party sites may resemble Sunbiz or charge additional fees for services that can be obtained through the official government website.

Researchers should verify that they are using an official Florida Department of State or Sunbiz domain.

## Recommended Research Procedure

1. Search by exact or partial entity name.
2. Review all similar search results.
3. Confirm the entity using its document number and other identifiers.
4. Record the entity type and state of domicile.
5. Review the filing status.
6. Compare the filing date and effective date.
7. Review the principal and mailing addresses separately.
8. Identify the registered agent without assuming ownership.
9. Review officer, director, or manager listings.
10. Check the most recent annual-report year.
11. Open the most recent relevant filed documents.
12. Review historical filings when changes matter.
13. Record the date the research was performed.
14. Phrase conclusions as statements about the official filing record.
15. Use other authoritative sources for licensing, operations, ownership, complaints, and regulated activities.
16. Document discrepancies rather than silently replacing conflicting information.

## Example of Appropriate Research Language

### Strong Wording

> The Florida Division of Corporations record lists ABC Example LLC as an active Florida limited liability company as of the review date.

> The company’s 2026 annual report identifies Jane Example as an authorized person.

> Sunbiz lists the principal address as 123 Example Street.

> The registered agent shown on the state record is Example Agent Services LLC.

### Wording to Avoid Without Additional Evidence

> ABC Example LLC is definitely operating.

> Jane Example owns the entire company.

> The address is a verified storefront.

> The company is fully licensed and compliant.

> The business has no complaints or legal problems.

> The registered agent runs the company.

## Final Assessment

**Overall reliability:** High for confirming Florida Division of Corporations records; Moderate for independently confirming filer-submitted facts

**Recommended use:** Primary source for Florida filing records, supplemented by other authoritative sources when the research concerns actual operations, licensing, ownership, complaints, taxes, or regulated activities

**Conclusion:**

Sunbiz is an authoritative and highly useful public-record source for determining what has been filed with the Florida Department of State, Division of Corporations.

Its strongest uses include:

- Confirming entity records
- Reviewing filing status
- Locating document numbers
- Identifying registered agents
- Reviewing listed officers or managers
- Examining annual-report history
- Downloading filed documents
- Tracing amendments and other corporate events

Its primary limitation is that it records filer-submitted information and does not independently establish the real-world accuracy of every statement.

Researchers should accurately describe Sunbiz findings as official filing-record information, preserve filing dates and context, and use additional authoritative sources when conclusions extend beyond the Division’s records.
