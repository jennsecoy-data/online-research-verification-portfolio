## Evaluation Criteria

### Authority

- Who published the information?
- Is the publisher qualified to provide it?
- Is the author or organization clearly identified?
- Is contact or ownership information available?

**Assessment:**  
High / Moderate / Low

**Notes:**  

### Accuracy

- Does the source provide evidence or citations?
- Can the information be confirmed elsewhere?
- Are claims presented clearly and without obvious errors?
- Does the source distinguish facts from opinions?

**Assessment:**  
High / Moderate / Low

**Notes:**  

### Currency

- Is a publication or update date provided?
- Is the information still current enough for the research purpose?
- Are links, policies, prices, or contact details still active?

**Assessment:**  
High / Moderate / Low

**Notes:**  

### Relevance

- Does the source directly answer the research question?
- Is the information specific enough to support the required conclusion?
- Is the geographic, legal, or industry context appropriate?

**Assessment:**  
High / Moderate / Low

**Notes:**  

### Transparency

- Does the source explain where its information came from?
- Are sponsorships, advertising, or conflicts of interest disclosed?
- Is the difference between editorial and promotional content clear?

**Assessment:**  
High / Moderate / Low

**Notes:**  

### Consistency

- Does the information agree with authoritative sources?
- Are important details consistent throughout the page?
- Were conflicting claims identified?

## Risks or Limitations

- Missing publication date
- Anonymous author
- Promotional bias
- Unsupported claims
- Outdated information
- Broken citations
- Geographic mismatch
- Information copied from another source
- Conflicts with authoritative records
