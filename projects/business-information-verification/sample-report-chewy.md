## Executive Summary

Chewy was verified as an active e-commerce company operating under the legal business name Chewy, Inc.

Authoritative sources consistently support the company name, official website, principal executive office, customer-service contact information, corporate email address, and primary business activities.

Two items required additional context:

1. Chewy publishes different phone numbers for customer service and corporate or regulatory purposes.
2. Public company materials reference both Plantation, Florida, and Boston, Massachusetts, as headquarters locations, while the company’s SEC filing identifies Plantation as its principal executive office.

These differences do not appear to be errors. They reflect separate business functions and location descriptions.

## Discrepancy and Context Log

### Headquarters Description

**Information found:**

- The SEC Form 10-K identifies 7700 West Sunrise Boulevard in Plantation, Florida, as Chewy’s principal executive office.
- Chewy’s careers website references corporate locations in both Plantation, Florida, and Boston, Massachusetts.

**Assessment:**

The information is not necessarily contradictory. “Principal executive office,” “headquarters,” and “corporate hub” may describe different operational functions.

**Resolution:**

Use the Plantation address when a record specifically requests the principal executive office or SEC-listed corporate address. Record Boston as an additional corporate location when relevant.

### Phone Numbers

**Information found:**

- Chewy’s customer-care page lists 1-800-672-4399.
- Chewy’s SEC filing lists 786-320-7111 as the registrant telephone number.

**Assessment:**

The numbers serve different purposes and should not be treated as interchangeable.

**Resolution:**

Use 1-800-672-4399 for customer-service records. Use 786-320-7111 only when documenting the SEC registrant or corporate filing information.

### Legal Location Versus Operating Location

**Information found:**

- Chewy, Inc. is incorporated in Delaware.
- Its principal executive office is located in Plantation, Florida.

**Assessment:**

The state of incorporation is a legal jurisdiction and does not necessarily represent the company’s physical headquarters.

**Resolution:**

Store the jurisdiction of incorporation and principal office address in separate database fields.

## Source Evaluation

### Authority

The SEC filing is the strongest source for the legal business name, jurisdiction of incorporation, principal executive office, registrant telephone number, and public-company information.

Chewy’s official websites are authoritative for customer contact information, products, services, and public location descriptions.

### Accuracy

Core information was consistent across the authoritative sources reviewed. Differences in addresses and phone numbers were explainable based on business purpose.

### Currency

The reviewed sources included Chewy’s annual filing for the fiscal year ending February 1, 2026, current investor information, and active official webpages accessed on August 4, 2026.

### Relevance

Each source was matched to the type of information it was best positioned to confirm. Customer-care information was not used as the sole source for legal-company data, and the SEC filing was not treated as the best source for routine customer support.
