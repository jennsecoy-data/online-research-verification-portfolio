## Executive Summary

CVS Health was verified as an active public health solutions company operating under the legal business name CVS Health Corporation.

Authoritative sources consistently support the company’s legal name, Delaware incorporation, principal executive office in Woonsocket, Rhode Island, public-company status, and New York Stock Exchange trading symbol.

The review identified two areas requiring careful data handling:

1. CVS Health, Aetna, CVS Caremark, CVS Pharmacy, and MinuteClinic should not be treated as interchangeable names. CVS Health Corporation is the legal parent organization, while the other names identify brands, services, or operations within the broader company.
2. CVS Health publishes different telephone numbers for different purposes. The SEC registrant number, general corporate headquarters number, and brand-specific customer-support channels should remain in separate fields.

The findings demonstrate why entity relationships and contact purpose must be documented before information is entered into a business database.

## Corporate Structure and Brand Relationship Review

### CVS Health Corporation

CVS Health Corporation is the legal parent entity and SEC registrant.

Use this name for records involving:

- Legal entity name
- Public-company filings
- State of incorporation
- Principal executive office
- Trading symbol and exchange
- Corporate governance
- Investor relations

### Reportable Business Segments

CVS Health’s SEC reporting identifies four reportable segments:

1. Health Care Benefits
2. Health Services
3. Pharmacy & Consumer Wellness
4. Corporate/Other

These segment names are more appropriate for financial and operational reporting than customer-facing brand names.

### Aetna

Aetna is part of CVS Health and provides health insurance and related benefit products.

Use Aetna-specific information for fields involving:

- Commercial health insurance
- Medicare plans
- Medicaid plans
- Dental and vision benefits
- Health-plan member services
- Insurance operations

Aetna should not automatically replace CVS Health Corporation in a legal parent-company field.

### CVS Caremark

CVS Caremark is CVS Health’s pharmacy benefit management operation.

Use CVS Caremark-specific information for fields involving:

- Pharmacy benefits
- Prescription drug coverage
- Mail-order pharmacy services
- Plan-member prescription support
- Employer or health-plan pharmacy administration

### CVS Pharmacy

CVS Pharmacy is CVS Health’s consumer-facing retail pharmacy brand.

Use CVS Pharmacy-specific information for fields involving:

- Retail pharmacy locations
- Prescription filling and pickup
- Prescription delivery
- Store customer service
- Vaccinations
- Retail health and wellness products

### MinuteClinic

MinuteClinic provides in-person and virtual health care services within CVS Health’s broader care-delivery model.

Use MinuteClinic-specific information for fields involving:

- Clinic services
- Walk-in or scheduled care
- Virtual care
- Preventive services
- Minor illness and injury treatment
- Selected primary-care services

## Discrepancy and Context Log

### Corporate Telephone Numbers

**Information found:**

- The SEC filing lists 401-765-1500 as the registrant telephone number.
- CVS Health’s public contact page lists 1-800-746-7287 with its corporate headquarters information.

**Assessment:**

The numbers appear in sources serving different purposes. The SEC number is formal registrant information, while the toll-free number is the company’s published general corporate contact.

The presence of both numbers does not indicate that either is inaccurate.

**Resolution:**

Store both telephone numbers with clear labels.

Recommended fields:

| Contact type | Telephone number |
|---|---|
| SEC registrant phone | 401-765-1500 |
| Published corporate headquarters phone | 1-800-746-7287 |

Do not overwrite one number with the other.

### Parent Company Versus Brands

**Information found:**

CVS Health publishes information under several well-known names, including:

- CVS Health
- Aetna
- CVS Caremark
- CVS Pharmacy
- MinuteClinic

**Assessment:**

These names represent different organizational levels, brands, services, and operations.

Treating the names as synonyms could create errors involving:

- Legal entities
- Customer-support routing
- Health insurance records
- Pharmacy benefit records
- Retail pharmacy records
- Clinic records
- Website and contact fields

**Resolution:**

Maintain separate fields for:

- Legal parent entity
- Brand name
- Business segment
- Service or operating unit
- Customer-support channel
- Official website

### Address Formatting

**Information found:**

- The SEC filing uses “One CVS Drive.”
- The public contact page uses “1 CVS Drive.”

Both sources identify Woonsocket, Rhode Island 02895.

**Assessment:**

The two versions represent the same location using different formatting.

This is a normalization issue rather than a meaningful address conflict.

**Resolution:**

Normalize the address only when the database has an established address-formatting standard. Preserve the original source version in the research notes.

### Aetna Relationship

**Information found:**

Official CVS Health materials identify Aetna as part of CVS Health. The company’s history records the completion of CVS Health’s acquisition of Aetna in 2018.

**Assessment:**

Aetna remains a recognizable health insurance brand within CVS Health. A record may correctly contain both names when the relationship is clearly labeled.

**Resolution:**

Use CVS Health Corporation for the legal parent entity.

Use Aetna for the applicable insurance brand, plan, or health-benefits operation.

### Customer-Support Routing

**Information found:**

CVS Health’s contact page directs customers to separate support resources for CVS Pharmacy, MinuteClinic, CVS.com, and CVS Caremark.

**Assessment:**

A general corporate contact is not necessarily the correct contact for a customer question involving prescriptions, insurance coverage, pharmacy benefits, clinics, or retail orders.

**Resolution:**

Select customer-support information based on the specific brand and requested service.

Do not treat the corporate headquarters number as a universal customer-service number.
