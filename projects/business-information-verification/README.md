# Business Information Verification

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates my ability to research and verify publicly available business information, compare findings across multiple sources, identify discrepancies, and document conclusions clearly.

## Scenario

A client needs reliable business information before updating an internal database. The goal is to confirm key details using current, publicly available sources and flag anything that cannot be verified confidently.

## Information Reviewed

- Business and brand name
- Legal business name
- Official website
- Business location
- Contact information
- Products or services
- Operating status
- Source consistency
- Date verified
- Confidence level

## Research Method

1. Review the company’s official website.
2. Locate authoritative registration or regulatory records when available.
3. Compare information across multiple independent sources.
4. Document matching, conflicting, missing, or outdated information.
5. Prioritize official and recently updated sources.
6. Assign a confidence level based on the quality and consistency of the evidence.

## Verification Standards

### Verified

The information is supported by authoritative sources and is consistent across the records reviewed.

### Partially Verified

Some information is supported, but additional confirmation may be needed.

### Unable to Verify

The available information is incomplete, inaccessible, outdated, or contradictory.

This project will include:

- A structured business verification worksheet
- Source links and access dates
- Discrepancy notes
- Confidence ratings
- A concise findings summary
