## Source Evaluation

Each source is evaluated using the following criteria:

- **Authority:** Who published the information?
- **Accuracy:** Is the information supported by other reliable sources?
- **Currency:** When was the information published or updated?
- **Relevance:** Does the source directly support the required data point?
- **Consistency:** Does the information agree with other authoritative sources?
