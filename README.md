## Research Approach

My research process generally includes:

1. Defining the research question and required data points.
2. Identifying authoritative, relevant, and current sources.
3. Collecting information using consistent criteria.
4. Comparing findings across multiple sources.
5. Recording discrepancies, missing information, and limitations.
6. Matching each data field to the source best qualified to support it.
7. Assigning confidence levels when appropriate.
8. Presenting findings in a clear and usable format.

## Source Standards

Sources are evaluated for:

- **Authority:** Who published the information?
- **Accuracy:** Can the information be independently confirmed?
- **Currency:** Is it current enough for the research purpose?
- **Relevance:** Does it directly support the required conclusion?
- **Transparency:** Is the source’s ownership, evidence, or purpose clear?
- **Consistency:** Does it agree with other authoritative information?

Government records, regulatory filings, and official organizational sources are prioritized when appropriate. Reference sites, directories, and user-generated content are treated as supporting sources or research leads unless independently verified.
